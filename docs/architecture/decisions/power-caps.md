| **Caps (project-wide)** | **A cap on a magnitude is a progression ceiling until proven otherwise (PS-8), and endless grind is the SSOT other systems reconcile *to*.** Progression ceilings are **removed or made configurable soft caps** — never hard stops: `ProgressionPowerCurve.MaxExponent` (class deleted with the curve, T3.2), `ContractPolicy.MaxSlots`, `SoulEarnPolicy.KillCapPerMatch`/`VictoryFullPerDay`, `PatronPolicy.KillSoulCap` (all four deleted, T3.6), PvZ drop caps (doc-only, D.3), the enhancement `+X` cap and the rarity promotion ceiling (doc-only, D.1/D.2) are all gone or soft. Absolute magnitude bounds are **derived and throw, never clamp silently**: `ShieldMath.MaxInput`, `ResourceDeltaMath.AmountCap`, `RpgStore.MaxSoulAwardFrom` (the last one **dynamic** — headroom to `long.MaxValue` from the live balance, not a constant at all — T3.5). The soul-earn formula (`soulsPerKill = KillDelta × contentScale(Θ)`, victory/defeat likewise) lands in the same commit as the cap deletions it replaces, byte-identical at Θ=20 (T3.6) — proven as a pure-formula stall-farm regression; the live vanilla-PvZ capture pipeline carrying a real per-kill `Θ` signal is a separate, unbuilt follow-up, not silently claimed as done. Exempt by nature: structural limits, bounded ratios, per-frame/runtime caps, retention tails — each carries a comment naming its class. **A ceiling need not be a `const`, nor be named like one:** an inline `Math.Min`, a narrowing `(int)` cast, a flat rate facing a scaling sink, and a threshold that halves a payout are all caps. Register: [power/ssot-power-scale.md](power/ssot-power-scale.md) §11. **Built 2026-08-24** (power-todo.md T3.5/T3.6). | <!-- decisions.md:62 -->
| **Magic numbers (project-wide)** | **The balance surface is data, not code.** A number a balance pass would change lives in `gk-core/data/tuning/<domain>.v{n}.json`; a structural constant stays a `const` and **says why it is not tunable**; `0`/`1`/`/2`/indices are literals. **Runtime catalogs** (displayName, readings, roster membership for player surfaces) live in sibling `gk-core/data/tuning/<domain>-catalog.v{n}.json` files — never mixed into the number file (T7 copy vs combat goldens). Policy, Catalog, Rules, Ruleset and Math files **are** the balance surface — no bare literals there. Config is versioned and never hand-edited; a missing tunable or catalog is a **load rejection naming it**, never a default. `Core` reads no file — hosts load and inject, as `IProgressionPowerProvider` already does. A tuning change must never be able to hide a code regression. SSOT: [tunables-ssot.md](tunables-ssot.md); audit: `python gk-core/scripts/audit-magic-numbers.py` (baseline 329 findings, 37 balance-surface files). | <!-- decisions.md:65 -->
