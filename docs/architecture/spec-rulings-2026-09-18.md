## Context the rulings rest on (verified 2026-09-18)

- **Gem words are planned deterministically; the model fills identity.** The grid is 12 aptitudes × 3
  archetypes (36 Strains) + C(12,2) (66 Splices) = 102 cells, read from `gk-data/packs/fusion/data/seed/aptitudes/roster.json` and
  the archetype registry (`combogen/grid.py:1-10`). The model picks the four ingredient families, the host
  role, the name and the flavour; the tier floors come from `strain-splice.v1.json` `minTierPlan`, and
  `minSockets` is computed.
- **Every one of the 76 generated words needs 4 sockets** (`gk-data/packs/fusion/data/seed/items/combinations/strains.json`,
  `splices.json`: `minSockets` 4 on every entry), hosted on `armament-primary` or `core-guard` only (2 Strains
  carry no host role).
