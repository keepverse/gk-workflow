the Garden Keeper's level

the Garden Keeper’s level is the main power line for your summoner — endless, with no cap. Play raises it; Vision chapters may drip complexity by it later.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

the Garden Keeper’s level
Your summoner’s level — the Garden Keeper’s power line — not a lawn plant’s XP bar alone.
Endless / no cap
There is no soft ceiling where grinding stops mattering by design. Advancing to a new world raises the challenge; it does not wipe who you are.
Aptitude points
Spendable power you earn as the Garden Keeper grows — see free-build aptitudes.
Chapters
Vision drip by the Garden Keeper level — not the live rail today. See unlock chapters.
  • Specimens and type lines also grow from work they did — see type and specimen XP.
  • HUD and Chronicle show progression; exact curves live in balance data.

What it is

The spine of the power loop: play → the Garden Keeper levels → spend into builds.

No designed hard stop where more play stops counting.

How you use it

  1. Play lawn, expeditions, and map work that feed progression.
  2. Watch the Garden Keeper’s level on the HUD / Chronicle.
  3. Spend aptitude points as you earn them — there is no soft ceiling telling you to stop.

What it is not

It is not the live unlock table for the rail (that is play beats).

It is not specimen XP — individuals grow on their own track too.

What you do (first time)

  1. Finish a lawn match or other play that pays experience.
  2. Find the Garden Keeper’s level on the HUD or Chronicle.
  3. If you have aptitude points, open builds and spend at least one.

Stop when “no cap” is clear. Next: type and specimen XP.

Common mix-ups

Is there a max the Garden Keeper level?
No designed cap. Endless grind is the rule.
Does a new world reset the Garden Keeper?
You keep who you are across worlds — see New world.
Why didn’t the Garden Keeper 10 open Relics?
the Garden Keeper chapters are Vision. Relics open on the live item beat.
Is the Garden Keeper’s level the same as a creature’s level?
No. Creatures have specimen XP; the Garden Keeper is the summoner line.
