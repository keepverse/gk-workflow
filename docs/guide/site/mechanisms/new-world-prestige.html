Advancing to a new world — keep who you are

You keep who you are. The worlds you leave keep going. Advancing to a new world is progression — not a prestige wipe of the summoner, and not the end of the world you left.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Advance
Moving on to a new, harder world — at any time. Roster, souls, and essence come with you; legions carry cargo up to a carry limit, larger after a win. Loam never crosses.
Vision status
Approved design (world-continuity, 2026-09-19), not yet in the build. Today’s build runs one world at a time.
Prestige wipe
The rejected idea that a new world deletes your summoner power. This game does not do that.
Old worlds
The world you leave does not end. It hibernates (catching up on End Turn) or, with a warden stationed, goes idle. It keeps its enemy empires and upkeep, and an unguarded one can fall.
  • Win: take the seat of the world’s dominant enemy empire — the world becomes developing and keeps running. Lose: enemy empires take your seat — that world becomes fallen, and your save continues.
  • Same roster, same souls, same essence — whichever world you stand on.

What it is

Advancing to a new world *is* the progression loop.

Identity (roster, souls, essence, the Garden Keeper’s power) banks. Place stays where it is: each world keeps its ground, structures and loam, and keeps running after you leave.

Comes with you Stays with the old world
Roster / specimens Loam (never crosses)
Souls, essence, materials Territory and structures
Legions and their cargo, up to the carry limit Anything past the carry limit; that world’s ground story

Status is Vision — the design is approved; advancing and old-world states are not in the build yet.

Why it exists

Without a keep/stay split, empire play either wipes your summoner (punishing) or piles up free ground forever (boring).

You keep who you are so power and roster mean something across worlds. Old worlds keep their pressure and upkeep, and pay back only a reduced, collected trickle, so holding them stays a real choice.

  advance (any time; carry more after a win)
        |
        +->  with you: roster, souls, essence, the Garden Keeper power, legions + cargo
        +->  stays: loam, ground, structures — the old world hibernates or idles

Where you meet it

Win and lose

Defined on The game — the dominant enemy empire’s seat vs your own.

  1. Play toward the dominant enemy empire’s seat — or hold your own.
  2. Advance when you choose; a win first lets your legions carry more.
  3. Open Chronicle / Creatures to confirm who you still are.

The advance dialog and world list are designed, not built.

What you do (first time)

  1. Read win/lose on The game once.
  2. Check that souls and essence are player banks — loam is not.
  3. Treat advancing as moving on, not a summoner delete — and not a delete of the world you left.

Stop when advancing ≠ soft reset of the Garden Keeper. Related: loam, souls.

Common mix-ups

Does a new world delete my creatures?
No. Roster banks. Your old world’s ground and loam stay with that world.
Do souls reset?
No. Souls and essence bank across worlds.
Can I take loam with me?
No. Loam never crosses worlds. Rubble and ironwork can, only as legion cargo.
Does the old world disappear?
No. It hibernates or idles, keeps its enemies and upkeep, and can fall if left unguarded.
Is this a paid prestige layer?
No. Play is local with no payments — advancing is the place loop.
