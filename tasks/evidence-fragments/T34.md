`plan.py`, `run.py`) — pure, deterministic, no model call. `species.py` gets its **own** family-map

here). Wired into `seedsmith items generate --kind trophy` (`report/cli.py`'s
`_PASSTHROUGH_MODULE_BY_KIND`), matching the existing passthrough shape rather than a new entry
point.
