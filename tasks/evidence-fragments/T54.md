## One honest deviation from the row's prose

The row says "re-plane the shipped sets onto the classes by **running the generator**". The mechanism
actually used is a **deterministic repair pass over the emitted corpus** (`topology_repair.py`, the same
shape `T28` used for `speciesId`), because re-running `set-charm-gen` would call a model for ~910 sets and
rewrite their *authored identity* (names, flavour) — a content pass, not a re-plan, and not something this
row's criteria ask for. What the row's criteria do ask for is preserved exactly: the corpus was produced
by code, not by hand; the diff is one field per entry; and the class is the ladder's own resolution for
each entry's declared topology.
